# Growth Audit — Routinery

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/routinery-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Routinery already has, each rebuilt as a working screen in a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall. Routinery's own design throughout: only the sequence changes.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Catch the “no” with a monthly plan: closing the paywall opens Free vs Premium at $5.00 a month, no trial | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Let people start before the account: “Start without signup” leads, sign-up moves after the paywall | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Put their routine on the paywall: a short build screen reads the answers back, the paywall names the routine and its time | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask for tracking after the paywall, not on the welcome screen | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show the yearly plan as $3.00 a month, 40% less than monthly | ARPU +10–15% |

The prototype has English, Korean and Japanese screens, light and dark mode, and a 30-second walkthrough. Open it on a phone with `?open=proto`.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, quote and award comes from Routinery's own App Store listing (US storefront), routinery.app and the in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Routinery.
